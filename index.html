About

Skillar is an independent app studio based in Ukraine, run by Andrii Ihnatiev (registered sole proprietor, FOP Ihnatiev Andrii). We build small, focused apps with no ads, no accounts and no tracking of personal data.

Our apps

  • Scriptkeep: memorize Scripture with spaced repetition.
  • Hitherto: a private prayer journal that remembers answered prayers.
  • Wordlamp: a calm Bible word search.
